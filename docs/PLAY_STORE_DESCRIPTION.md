# SafeMobile Antivirus — Google Play Store Description

**100% Offline Malware Scanning. Ad-supported Free Tier. No Ads or Tracking with Pro.**

SafeMobile performs its core malware scanning on your device. It checks readable installed APKs and files you select against local threat definitions and performs supported static inspection. A no-match result is not a guarantee that a file is safe.

## Free tier
The Free tier is supported by Google AdMob. Banner ads appear on the Home dashboard, and an interstitial may appear after a successful manual scan when no confirmed threats are found. Google AdMob may collect the Android Advertising ID and IP addresses for ad delivery, ad measurement/reporting and analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's UMP privacy choices are refreshed at app launch and required consent forms are shown before AdMob initializes or requests ads.

## SafeMobile Pro
While SafeMobile Pro is active, AdMob is not initialized and no ads are requested or displayed. Pro users are exempt from AdMob advertising and associated AdMob data collection. Pro entitlement is verified locally on this device. Optional definition updates, user-initiated Google Play purchases and any explicitly enabled Global Threat Intelligence contribution remain separately disclosed network features.

SafeMobile is not a kernel-level security product and cannot inspect every private file or guarantee detection of every malware family. Coverage depends on Android access, local definitions and device restrictions.
