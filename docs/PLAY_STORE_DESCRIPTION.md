# SafeMobile Antivirus — Google Play Store Description

**On-device Malware Scanning. Ad-supported Free Tier. No AdMob Ads with Pro.**

SafeMobile performs its core malware scanning on your device. It checks readable installed APKs and files you select against local threat definitions and performs supported static inspection. A no-match result is not a guarantee that a file is safe.

## Free tier
The Free tier is supported by Google AdMob. Banner ads appear on the Home dashboard, and an interstitial may appear after a successful manual scan when no confirmed threats are found. Google's Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK automatically collects and shares IP address; user product interactions (including app launches, taps and video views); diagnostic information (including app launch time, hang rate and energy usage); and device/account identifiers (including Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts) for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app does not send scanned APK contents, scan verdicts or local scan history to AdMob. Google's UMP refreshes privacy choices at app launch and presents any required consent forms before ad requests.

## SafeMobile Pro
While SafeMobile Pro is active, AdMob is not initialized and no ads are requested or displayed. Pro users are exempt from AdMob advertising and associated AdMob data collection. Pro entitlement is verified locally on this device. Optional definition updates, user-initiated Google Play purchases and any explicitly enabled Global Threat Intelligence contribution remain separately disclosed network features.

SafeMobile is not a kernel-level security product and cannot inspect every private file or guarantee detection of every malware family. Coverage depends on Android access, local definitions and device restrictions.
